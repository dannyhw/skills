**Resolved: Elevated API errors on 3 March 2026**

Between 14:02 and 14:31 UTC on 3 March 2026, around 12% of API requests failed with HTTP 502 errors.

**Cause:** A deploy at 14:00 UTC removed the cache warmup step. Without it, our servers could not handle normal traffic, and some requests failed.

**Resolution:** We reverted the deploy at 14:27 UTC. Error rates fell back to normal by 14:31 UTC, and the service has been stable since.

**Next steps:** We are changing our deploy checks so that removing the cache warmup step is flagged before release. We will also add an alert that catches this kind of error spike sooner.

We apologise for the disruption. If you still see problems, please contact our support team.
