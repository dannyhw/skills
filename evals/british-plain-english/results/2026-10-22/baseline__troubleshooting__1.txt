## Troubleshooting

### Postgres connection timeouts

If `sqlpipe` hangs or fails with `connection timed out`, first confirm the database is reachable from your machine: `pg_isready -h <host> -p 5432`. Check that your firewall, VPN, or security group allows inbound traffic on the Postgres port. Verify the host, port, and credentials in your connection string, and make sure `sslmode` matches the server's requirements. For slow networks or large queries, raise the timeout with `--connect-timeout 30`. If you connect through a pooler such as PgBouncer, confirm it hasn't reached its maximum client connections.

### S3 permission errors

An `AccessDenied` or `403 Forbidden` error means your credentials lack the required permissions. Run `aws sts get-caller-identity` to confirm which identity `sqlpipe` is using. The IAM policy must allow `s3:GetObject`, `s3:PutObject`, and `s3:ListBucket` on the target bucket and prefix. Also check for restrictive bucket policies, a mismatched region (set with `--region`), and expired session tokens. Re-authenticate if you use temporary credentials.
