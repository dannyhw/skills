**Appearance**

Change how the app looks and feels. Your choices save automatically and apply on every device you sign in to.

**Theme**
Choose Light, Dark, or System. System follows your device settings and switches between light and dark on its own. You can preview each theme before you commit to it.

**Accent colour**
The accent colour is used for buttons, links, and highlights. Pick one of the preset colours, or enter your own hex code, such as #3366FF. If a colour is hard to read against your theme, we will show a warning. Select Reset to return to the default.

**Sidebar on narrow windows**
Decide what the sidebar does when your window is small. Choose Collapse to icons to keep it visible but slim. Choose Hide to give your content the full width. You can open the sidebar at any time with the menu button.
