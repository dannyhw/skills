**Resolved: Elevated API Errors on 3 March 2026**

Between 14:02 and 14:31 UTC on 3 March 2026, approximately 12% of API requests failed with HTTP 502 errors.

**Cause:** A deployment at 14:00 UTC unintentionally removed the cache warmup step. Without a warmed cache, some requests could not be served successfully and returned errors.

**Resolution:** We identified the problem and reverted the deployment at 14:27 UTC. Error rates returned to normal by 14:31 UTC, and the service has been stable since.

**Next steps:** We are updating our deployment process so that the cache warmup step is validated before release. We are also improving our monitoring to detect this kind of failure sooner.

We apologize for the disruption and appreciate your patience.
