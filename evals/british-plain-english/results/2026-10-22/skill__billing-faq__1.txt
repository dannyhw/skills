## What happens when your team cancels its subscription

Your team keeps full access until the end of the current billing period. Cancelling does not end your licence straight away, and you do not get a refund for the days that remain.

When you cancel, the following happens:

1. Your licence stays active until the last day of the billing period. Everyone on the team can carry on working until then.
2. Cancelled seats are removed. After the billing period ends, those users can no longer sign in to the team's workspace.
3. We keep your data for 30 days after the billing period ends. During this time, you can export it or restart your subscription to get it back.

If you do not restart your subscription within 30 days, we permanently delete your data. We cannot recover it after that.

To keep a copy of your work, export your data before the billing period ends. You can do this from the account settings page.
